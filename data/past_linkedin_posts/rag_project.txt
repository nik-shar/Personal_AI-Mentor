I just shipped a production RAG pipeline — no vector database required.

Most tutorials tell you you need ChromaDB or Pinecone before you can do retrieval. I wanted to see how far BM25 + a cross-encoder reranker could take me before reaching for a vector store.

Turns out: pretty far.

The pipeline handles a 10k-document corpus, returns relevant chunks in under 200ms, and costs nothing to host. The cross-encoder step eliminates most of the noise BM25 lets through.

Is it better than dense retrieval in every case? No. But for keyword-heavy domains it matches or beats it — and the infra story is dramatically simpler.
